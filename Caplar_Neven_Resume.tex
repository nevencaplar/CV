\section*{Professional Summary}

Scientific data systems lead with 9+ years of experience building data platforms for large
astronomical surveys. Lead the open-source HATS/LSDB format and toolkit for cross-matching and
analyzing billion-row catalogs, used for data from Rubin Observatory and other large surveys.
Co-PI on a U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Genesis Mission project building a multimodal data service for AI model training.

\section*{Experience}

\textbf{University of Washington / LINCC Frameworks} \hfill \textit{2022 -- Present} \\
\textit{Research Scientist/Engineer}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Scientific lead and project owner of HATS/LSDB: a survey-agnostic, Parquet-based format and
        Python toolkit for scalable analysis and cross-matching of billion-object catalogs.
  \item Own the technical roadmap and guide architecture for distributed (Dask) catalog analysis;
        extending the platform to upcoming surveys (Roman, Euclid, Gaia DR4, Schmidt Observatory System).
  \item Co-PI, DOE Genesis Mission award (with Carnegie Mellon University and SLAC): unified multimodal
        data service (catalogs, images, spectra) and streaming data loaders for AI model training.
  \item Lead a team of 8 software engineers and scientists (UW, CMU); coordinate development
        with NASA archives (STScI/MAST, IPAC/IRSA), international data centers, and survey teams.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Vera C. Rubin Observatory} \hfill \textit{2022 -- 2025} \\
\textit{Project Scientist}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Contributed to commissioning and validation of time-domain pipelines,
        including difference imaging and large-scale light-curve construction.
  \item Defined scientific requirements and validation strategies for pipelines
        processing nightly multi-terabyte data volumes; co-author of the Data Preview 1 paper.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Princeton University} \hfill \textit{2017 -- 2022} \\
\textit{Associate Professional Specialist}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Built the 2D point-spread-function model for the Prime Focus Spectrograph (Subaru Telescope)
        data reduction pipeline, combining physical modeling and machine-learning methods.
\end{itemize}
